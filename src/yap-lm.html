Young Athletes Program Resources

A sport and play program for children with and without intellectual disabilities (ID), ages 2 to 7 years old.

Introduction to Young Athletes Program

Young Athletes Program welcomes children and their families in the Special Olympics Sarawak by showing how all children can be valued for their talents and abilities. This page contains introductory information of this program and activity guide which provides activities to run Young Athletes Program in schools, communities and homes.
