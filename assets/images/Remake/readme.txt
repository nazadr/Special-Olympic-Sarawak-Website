Typography for "Special Olympics" = Serpentine Italic Bold
Typography for "Sarawak" = Ubuntu (italic regular)

This remake logo is the proposed official logo where typography is used like the international and other national Special Olympics, including Special Olympics Malaysia.
Unlike the existing logo which used the Shrikhand font for city chapters and "Sarawak" logo title, which are not intended or is commonly used by other Special Olympics.
Images contain transparent background include "BG" in the file name.

Ubuntu font is free for personal and commercial use, and is available on Google Font.

Shrikhand font is free for personal and commercial use, and is available on Google Font.

Serpentine Italic Bold font is part of the Serpentine family, and is typically requires to purchase a license for commercial use.
